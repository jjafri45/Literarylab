Who We Are

Publishing Professionals Who Care

Literary Lab is a dedicated book publishing and design studio operating under Zorq Studio. We work exclusively with self-published and independent authors — people with powerful stories who deserve the same production quality as traditional publishing houses.

Our team combines deep expertise in book design, typography, and publishing standards to deliver results that make authors genuinely proud. We've helped over 200 authors publish across Amazon KDP, IngramSpark, Apple Books, and more.
